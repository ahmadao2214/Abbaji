## Project knowledge wiki

For research, source ingestion, and questions about this project, read `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/SCHEMA.md` first. Use the installed `project-llm-wiki` skill when setting up, ingesting, or maintaining this wiki. Every user-facing wiki invocation requires a fresh Jev evaluation and the skill completion check; structural helpers alone do not establish completion. Keep immutable observations in `raw/`; maintain cited knowledge in `wiki/`. After collecting new research, snapshot the results, update affected wiki pages and the registry/index, append the log, and run wiki lint. Preserve evidence dates and uncertainty; an imported note is not a freshly read source. This block applies to knowledge work and does not replace other project instructions.
