# Branding Brief
## Abdul Aziz Butt Memorial Website

Hi! Welcome to the branding work for Abbaji's memorial website. This document outlines what we need help with.

---

## 📋 Project Overview

**Project:** Memorial website for Abdul Aziz Butt (1916-2016)
**Who was he:** Founding father of University of Kashmir, educator, social worker
**Personality:** "Ever-smiling, soft spoken, meticulously dressed and a gentleman to the core"
**Legacy:** Taught thousands, one of only 14 from his village to become educated

---

## 🎯 Your Mission

Help create a **cohesive visual brand identity** that:
1. Honors a distinguished academic and social worker
2. Feels authentically Kashmiri (not generic "South Asian")
3. Works across web, print, and social media
4. Is timeless, not trendy

## 🤔 Questions to Guide Your Work

### Brand Personality
On a scale of 1-5, where should this brand land?

1. **Traditional ←→ Modern**
   - 1 = Very traditional Kashmiri/Mughal style
   - 5 = Contemporary/minimal
   - Current: ~2.5-3

2. **Ornate ←→ Minimal**
   - 1 = Rich patterns, detailed embellishments
   - 5 = Clean lines, lots of white space
   - Current: ~3

3. **Warm ←→ Cool**
   - 1 = Warm burgundy/gold/orange emphasis
   - 5 = Cool blue/grey emphasis
   - Current: ~2 (warm)

4. **Academic ←→ Personal**
   - 1 = Formal university memorial
   - 5 = Intimate family tribute
   - Current: ~2.5 (balanced)

**Your input:** Where would YOU place it based on the content?
