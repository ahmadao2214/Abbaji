# Open confirmations

This page lists only the items that still need a document or a family check. Settled points are not repeated here. They include Abbaji, the lost thumb, Masooda's name, the professorship itself, the Registrar's dates, the date and place of death, and the claims to remove. They also include two points the family settled on 11 November 2026: the birth date the site leads with (26 July 1916), and that he was the first educated person from his village ([family statements](../../research/2026-11-11-family/statements.md)). All of these are on the [entity page](../entities/abdul-aziz-bhat.md). The site wording to use until each item below is settled is in [site content updates](../topics/site-content-updates.md).

Jev checked several of these items on 11 November 2026 ([decision run](../../research/wiki-decisions/2026-11-11-jev-followup.json)). Its answers are noted where they bear on an item. They are judgments about the passages supplied, not evidence.

## 1. The surname on the Calendar line: three university files

**Status.** The university's *Calendar 2010* lists "4. Prof. Abdul Aziz — Sept1962 – 1971" among its Registrars, with no surname ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)). The family knows this is Abbaji. The link to the surname Butt/Bhat rests on several books and an obituary agreeing ([report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). No university document yet names him in full.

Two more records made at the time now agree with the books, and neither gives a surname ([Oxford report, F6](../../research/2026-11-11-oxford/report.md)):
- "Shri Abdul Aziz, Registrar, Kashmir University", met by a national committee in Srinagar in August 1970.
- "Prof. Abdul Aziz, Principal, M. E. T. Teachers' College, Sopore", in the university's 1975–76 annual report. Jev rated this line insufficient to identify him, even with the books' Sopore passages ([decision run, S4b](../../research/wiki-decisions/2026-11-11-jev-followup.json)).

**The three files that would confirm it** ([report: Conclusion](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [notes: documentary-gap, Q5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)):

1. **The September 1962 appointment order.** It would give his full name, the exact post and the date he started.
2. **The 1971 relieving (or handover) order.** It would fix the end date. It would also show whether he was Registrar or Joint Registrar during 1965–69, when the university had a Kashmir Division ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1 inferences](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).
3. **The first page of his service book.** It would give his name, father's name, date of birth and home address. Matched to Kilhotran and the family's records, it ties "Prof. Abdul Aziz" to Abdul Aziz Butt. The same page would settle item 4 and confirm the 26 July birth date. The service book's list of posts bears on items 2, 7 and 10, and may record a foreign deputation (item 3).

**Where.** The Registrar, University of Kashmir, Hazratbal, Srinagar 190006, General Administration / Establishment section. Ask through the university's RTI Information Cell, or by a legal heir's letter enclosing the death certificate and proof of relationship. The notes judge the heir's letter the stronger route for a personal file ([notes: documentary-gap, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).

**Until then.** The site may say he was Registrar from September 1962 to 1971. The family's knowledge and the Calendar's dates support that.

## 2. The professorship: department and appointment paper

**Status.** He was a professor at the University of Kashmir *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The Calendar, two local histories and a 2021 Greater Kashmir column all give him the title Professor. None names a department or subject ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [education first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The professorship is not in doubt. The **department** and the **appointment paper** are unconfirmed.

**What would settle it.** The order or letter appointing him professor, or the service book's record of posts (file 3 above). Family papers would also help: letters on university letterhead, or a printed programme naming his chair.

**Until then.** "Professor", with no subject named.

## 3. Oxford: year, college or certificate

**Status.** He was a travelling scholar at Oxford *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. A dedicated search on 11 November 2026 found no source naming him at Oxford, as a travelling scholar, or on any British award list ([Oxford report](../../research/2026-11-11-oxford/report.md)). That reflects what is online. The 1950s Oxford University Gazette cannot be searched online, and the likely archives are not digitised. It is not evidence against the family's account.

The record does have a separate fact: a **one-year training course in England in 1956**, with Ghulam Rasool Azad and others. KBA's version, from his own interview, says "mid-fifties". Neither source names an institution. The course does not stand in for Oxford, and Jev found that the course passages do not address Oxford ([decision run, S2](../../research/wiki-decisions/2026-11-11-jev-followup.json)).

**What the research learned about the letters.** "H.P." is probably Honours in Persian, which is Munshi Fazil, not a British award. That is an inference ([decision run, S6](../../research/wiki-decisions/2026-11-11-jev-followup.json)). "E.E.T&P (London)" is still unexplained ([Oxford report, C1–C2](../../research/2026-11-11-oxford/report.md)).

**What would settle it.** Any one of the **year**, the **college** or a **certificate**. The leads, best first ([Oxford report: Leads](../../research/2026-11-11-oxford/report.md)):

1. **Family papers.** A certificate, a passport for the mid-1950s or 1960–62, letters, a dated photograph, or the service book (file 3), which normally records foreign deputations. Ghulam Rasool Azad's family may hold papers for the same 1956 course.
2. **The J&K order that sent him to England in 1955–56.** Also the J&K Gazette for 1955–57. Ask the J&K Archives, Srinagar (Old Secretariat) or Jammu (Mubarak Mandi).
3. **The National Archives, Kew.**
   - OD 3/5, "Commonwealth Teaching Training Bursary Scheme: India", 1960–65 (open). Under this scheme some teachers trained at Oxford from 1960 (Hansard, 17 May 1960).
   - BW 38/19 and BW 38/22, the British Council's India reports, 1955–61.
   - ED 159/141–142, the Oxford Institute of Education.
4. **Oxford University Archives** (university.archives@bodleian.ox.ac.uk). Ask about the Department of Educational Studies records for 1955–62, and about who the closed card register of students (RS 42) covers.
5. **UCL Institute of Education Archives**, for the London side: IE/COL/20/1 (Commonwealth bursars, 1960–66) and IE/OSD/2/A/2 (fellowships for India, 1952–76). These may also explain "E.E.T&P (London)".

**Until then.** "The family remembers him as a travelling scholar at Oxford." Give no date, and do not call him a guest lecturer.

## 4. His father's name

**Status.** Azhar remembers it as **Muhammad Sultan** *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The English scan of *The History and Culture of Bhadarwah* prints "Khwaja Sulla Bhat" (p. 774). The research quoted the OCR text layer, not the page image ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2 and source key](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)), so this may be a misreading. Two other men's names could mislead a search. The book's dedication names the author's father, Khawaja Mohammad Sultan Khateeb *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. Its lists also include a "Professor Sultan Muhammad Khan" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). Jev rated the family's name and the OCR name a conflict over the same fact ([decision run, R3](../../research/wiki-decisions/2026-11-11-jev-followup.json)). So one of them is wrong; Jev's rating does not say which.

**What would settle it.** Any one of:
- the page image of p. 774 in the Internet Archive item `the-history-and-culture-of-bhadarwah`, read by eye. The Oxford search read p. 775 from its image (leaf n826), so p. 774 should be the leaf before;
- the Urdu original, *Bhaderwah ki Tareekh o Saqafat*, which the research did not find;
- the service book's first page (file 3).

**Until then.** Do not print "Sulla". If the site names his father, use the family's name and say it is the family's.

## 11. The surname in Urdu

**Status.** The site's English has read "Butt" since the family's correction of 6 January 2026 (commit `f439b8c`). The hero's Urdu is عبدالعزیز بھٹ ([index.astro, line 19](../../src/pages/index.astro)).

**Is بھٹ "Butt"? No.** بھ is the aspirated *bh*, so بھٹ reads *Bhaṭ*, the same letters as the start of Bhutto (بھٹو). In Urdu, the Punjabi and Kashmiri surname spelled Butt in English is normally written بٹ (b + ṭ). The Kashmiri surname Bhat is usually written بٹ as well. So بٹ suits both of the family's English spellings, and بھٹ suits neither. *(Inference from Urdu orthography. No source in the research shows how he wrote his own name.)*

**What would settle it.** A family check: his signature or his name in Urdu on a letter, a document or a gravestone.

**Until then.** Leave the Urdu as it is. Once the family confirms, change it to عبدالعزیز بٹ.
