# Site content updates

This is a plan for correcting the memorial site's text. It does not change `src/`. It covers the five pages in `src/pages/` and the two shared files that repeat the same claims, [Footer.astro](../../src/components/Footer.astro) and [BaseLayout.astro](../../src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro). Line numbers refer to the working tree of branch `claude/kashmir-memorial-site-SEb3t` as read on 11 November 2026. The facts behind each change are set out, with citations, on the [entity page](../entities/abdul-aziz-bhat.md). Items still waiting on a document or a family check are on [open confirmations](../questions/open-confirmations.md).

Citation shorthand used in the tables. Every link points to a research file from 11 November 2026:

- **report**: [report.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md), with the section named
- **person Qn**: [notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)
- **first pass**: [notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)
- **gap Qn**: [notes/documentary-gap.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)
- **edu Qn**: [notes/education-and-registrar.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-and-registrar.md)
- **founding §n**: [notes/founding.md](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md)
- **family, 11 Nov**: Azhar's statements of 11 November 2026, which are family knowledge

## Rules applied on every page

1. **Remove** the claims the record does not support. These are founding faculty in 1948, building the university "from the rock" (the physical construction and the Education curriculum), Dean of Education, a PhD, professorships in named subjects (English, Social Work, Education), pioneering social work education at the university, Registrar "in the 1970s", study at the Bhaderwah degree college, and guest lectures at Warwick. Basis:
   - The 1948 body was an examining institute, and teaching began in 1956 ([founding §1–2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md)).
   - The Civil Lists keep him in the school cadre to about 1953 ([report: Education department](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
   - The Department of Education dates itself to 1965 and was headed by Dr. Ashraf Agha, also known as Agha Ashraf Ali ([gap Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md); [edu Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-and-registrar.md)).
   - Social Work began in 2003 ([person Q6](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
   - No source found a doctorate or a department ([report: Education department](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
   - The phrase "from the rock" appears in no source opened ([founding §5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md)).
   - The Calendar gives the Registrar's term as September 1962–1971 ([gap Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).
2. **Keep** family knowledge, worded as the family's:
   - Abbaji.
   - The professorship, with no department named.
   - Oxford, as a travelling scholar, with no date and not as "guest lecturer".
   - Masooda's name and his lost thumb.
   - That he was the first educated person from his village, and that he taught himself to read. This replaces the site's count of fourteen, which the family corrected on 11 November 2026 ([family statements](../../research/2026-11-11-family/statements.md)).
   - His work with young people.
   - "Sink your differences and Cement your relationships."
3. **Add** the plain, documented facts that the inflated claims had crowded out: his posts from 1941, the Registrar years, Sopore, and his death in Jammu on 7 August 2016 ([entity page](../entities/abdul-aziz-bhat.md)).
4. **Hold** anything still waiting on a check: Warwick, the GGM lectureship, Masooda's age, the Urdu spelling of the surname, and three quotations ([open confirmations](../questions/open-confirmations.md)).
5. **Voice.** Use short, plain sentences. Avoid superlatives such as "extraordinary", "remarkable", "prestigious" and "world's finest". DESIGN.md asks for a tone that is "respectful, dignified, warm" and avoids "overly dramatic or sensationalized language". Its rule is to mark what is unverified. The new wording marks it inside the sentence ("the family remembers") instead of in public `[To be corroborated]` or `[Note: …]` brackets. The grammar patterns to fix are listed under [Grammar and phrasing](#grammar-and-phrasing).

## Grammar and phrasing

Much of the current copy was drafted by earlier AI models, and it shows. The same faults recur on every page. Most of the lines above already have replacement wording, and that wording fixes them. This section names the patterns, so that anyone editing the site, or writing new copy, can recognise and avoid them. It ends with the lines that no table above covers. Line numbers are as read on 11 November 2026.

### Patterns

| Pattern | Examples on the site | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Fragments with no subject or verb.** Card and timeline text written like notes. | "Taught himself to read as a child in Bhalessa village." (index 90) · "Founding faculty member of the University of Kashmir in 1948."
