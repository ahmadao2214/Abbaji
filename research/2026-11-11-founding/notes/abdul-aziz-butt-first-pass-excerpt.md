# Abdul Aziz Butt notes, first pass (kept before a later rewrite)

`abdul-aziz-butt.md` was later rewritten. The rewrite says web searches of Kashmir Life and other papers found no 2016 obituary, and it treats a 7 April 2017 KNS notice of "Master Abdul Aziz Bhat" as an unresolved death date. The first pass had already opened a Kashmir Life obituary and a book containing his own interview. This file keeps only what was read from that first version. It is not a new search.

## Sources the first pass opened

- S1. Muhammad Raafi, "#Day31: Witnessed 1947 Massacre Of Jammu Muslims, KU Ex-Registrar Dies At 98", *Kashmir Life*, 8 August 2016. https://kashmirlife.net/day31-witnessed-1947-massacre-of-jammu-muslims-ku-ex-registrar-dies-at-98-114045/ The article says the information came from Jalal Ud Din Shah.
- S2. Khalid Bashir Ahmad, *Kashmir: A Walk Through History*, including an interview footnoted as "Abdul Aziz Bhat in an interview with the author on [day garbled] December 2011." https://archive.org/details/burzahom-kashmirawalkthroughhistorykhalidbashira-69086
- S3. Bashir Bhadarwahi, *The History & Culture of Bhadarwah* (2023), profile at pp. 774–776. https://archive.org/details/the-history-and-culture-of-bhadarwah
- S4. Sadaket Ali Malik, *Bhalessa* (2021). The first pass judged this **not independent**: it copies S1 and S2. https://archive.org/details/b-h-a-l-e-s-s-a-by-sadaket-malik
- S6. University of Kashmir, *Sixth Annual Report* (1975–76), naming "Prof./Shri Abdul Aziz" with no surname. https://archive.org/details/dli.ernet.509430

The first pass also listed "Master Abdul Aziz Bhat" among **other men** in S3, not as the Registrar.

## Quotations read from that first version

- "Professor Abdul Aziz Bhat was born in the renowned Bhat family of village Kilhotran Tehsil Bhalesa, to Khwaja Sulla Bhat on 26 July 1916 AD." S3, p. 774.
- S2 says he was born on 26 July 1916 and "passed away on 7 August 2016, twelve days after he had completed 100 years of his life."
- S1: "He was 98 and breathed his last in Jammu on Sunday." "Aziz lived in Gogjibagh area in Srinagar." "He was born at Kilhotran village in Gondu Tehsil near Thatri ahead of Doda on Kishtwar road."
- S2: "Abdul Aziz Bhat, Head Master of Hari Singh High School and later Registrar of Jammu & Kashmir University."
- S3: "He graciously served the chair of Registrar Jammu and Kashmir University from 1962-1969 AD. After the branching out of Jammu University, he continued serving as a Registrar for Kashmir University. He retired from Government services in the year 1974 AD."
- S2: Registrar of the Jammu and Kashmir University between 1962 and 1969 and later of Kashmir University; also Secretary of the Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education, Principal of S. P. College, and Inspector of Schools; after retirement in 1974, Principal of the Muslim Educational Trust, Sopore, until 1981.
- S1: "He is survived by three sons and three daughters. His son, Javid Ahmad, is first Muslim commercial pilot of Kashmir. Another son, Altaf Ahmad, retired as executive engineer and third son is a doctor."
- S3, p. 671: "Jenab Abdul Aziz Butt former Registrar Jammu and Kashmir University, who is also the first M.A. of this place."

The first pass's own takeaway, as read in the file, was that Hari Singh High School was confirmed in his interview account as Head Master from 1946, and that a GGM Science College lectureship was not found.

## How to use this with the rewrite

The rewrite adds the 1947 and about-1952 Civil Lists, which the report should keep. Those lists are primary. They do not cancel S1 or S2. A search that missed https://kashmirlife.net/day31-witnessed-1947-massacre-of-jammu-muslims-ku-ex-registrar-dies-at-98-114045/ is a missed page, not evidence that the obituary does not exist. The 7 April 2017 KNS notice of "Master Abdul Aziz Bhat" gives no posts and should not replace the 7–8 August 2016 death in S1 and S2.
