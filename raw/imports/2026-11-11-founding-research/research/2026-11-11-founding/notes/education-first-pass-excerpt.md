## Department of Education

- The department page said the Department of Education was established in 1965 and began with a one-year M.Ed. A two-year M.A. Education followed in 1979, and M.Phil. and Ph.D. work from the early 1980s. Source cited: https://education.uok.edu.in/Main/AboutUs.aspx
- Kashmir Life, 26 Aug 2020, "Agha Ashraf Ali (1922-2020)", said he was Inspector of Schools, Kashmir, in 1952; officiating principal of the Teachers College of Education from 1954 to 1960; and a professor who started heading the Education Department in 1968. The article's author is described as a relative. Source cited: https://kashmirlife.net/agha-ashraf-ali-1922-2020-issue-21-vol-12-243771/
- Kashmir Times, 6 Aug 2024, credits Agha Ashraf Ali with establishing (1968) and consolidating the department. Source cited: https://kashmirtimes.com/opinion/comment-articles/wit-wisdom-and-oratory-kashmirs-beloved-and-eloquent-scholar
- Greater Kashmir, Showkat Rashid Wani, 17 Jan 2021, "Lest we forget", calls Agha Ashraf Ali the "founder Dean & Head Post Graduate department of Education" and mentions "the table he had procured in Dean chamber of Education department in 1967". Source cited: https://www.greaterkashmir.com/opinion/lest-we-forget-6/

## Registrar named Abdul Aziz, without a surname or years

The same Greater Kashmir column was quoted as saying that **Prof. Abdul Aziz** "served as the Registrar & Controller of the Jammu & Kashmir University". No years and no surname Butt, Bhat, or Bhatt were in the quotation. The first pass also said no opened source tied any named Registrar to the Education department.

Other registrar names in that first pass, for comparison:

- Ghulam Ahmad Ashai, first Registrar, 1948–1953, from Wikipedia (uncited there) and the same Greater Kashmir column.
- Prof. Saif-ud-din Soz, named as Registrar at a seminar on 13 April 1988 in that column, which conflicts with a Lok Sabha term of 1983–1991.
- Prof. A.M. Mattoo, Prof. G.N. Sidiqi, and Prof. Abdul Rashid Mattoo, from an alumni list and a 2025 obituary, without usable years for the 1953–1988 gap.
