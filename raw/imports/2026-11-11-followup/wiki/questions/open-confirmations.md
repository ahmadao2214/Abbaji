# Open confirmations

This page lists only the items that still need a document or a family check. Settled points are not repeated here: Abbaji, the lost thumb, Masooda's name, the professorship itself, the Registrar's dates, the date and place of death, and the claims to remove. They are on the [entity page](../entities/abdul-aziz-bhat.md). The site wording to use until each item is settled is in [site content updates](../topics/site-content-updates.md).

## 1. The surname on the Calendar line: three university files

**Status.** The university's *Calendar 2010* lists "4. Prof. Abdul Aziz — Sept1962 – 1971" among its Registrars, with no surname ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)). The family knows this is Abbaji. The link to the surname Butt/Bhat rests on several books and an obituary agreeing ([report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). No university document yet names him in full.

**The three files that would confirm it** ([report: Conclusion](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [notes: documentary-gap, Q5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)):

1. **The September 1962 appointment order.** It would give his full name, the exact post and the date he started.
2. **The 1971 relieving (or handover) order.** It would fix the end date. It would also show whether he was Registrar or Joint Registrar during 1965–69, when the university had a Kashmir Division ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1 inferences](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).
3. **The first page of his service book.** It would give his name, father's name, date of birth and home address. Matched to Kilhotran and the family's records, it ties "Prof. Abdul Aziz" to Abdul Aziz Butt. The same page would also settle items 4 and 5 below, and the service book's list of posts bears on item 2.

**Where.** The Registrar, University of Kashmir, Hazratbal, Srinagar 190006, General Administration / Establishment section. Ask through the university's RTI Information Cell, or by a legal heir's letter enclosing the death certificate and proof of relationship. The notes judge the heir's letter the stronger route for a personal file ([notes: documentary-gap, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).

**Until then.** The site may say he was Registrar from September 1962 to 1971. The family's knowledge and the Calendar's dates support that.

## 2. The professorship: department and appointment paper

**Status.** He was a professor at the University of Kashmir *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The Calendar, two local histories and a 2021 Greater Kashmir column all give him the title Professor. None names a department or subject ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [education first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The professorship is not in doubt. The **department** and the **appointment paper** are unconfirmed.

**What would settle it.** The order or letter appointing him professor, or the service book's record of posts (file 3 above). Family papers would also help: letters on university letterhead, or a printed programme naming his chair.

**Until then.** "Professor", with no subject named.

## 3. Oxford: year, college or certificate

**Status.** He was a travelling scholar at Oxford *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. No source the research opened mentions Oxford ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q7](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). The record does have a training course in England in 1956, with Ghulam Rasool Azad and others (Bhadarwah history, printed pp. 774–775). That is a separate fact and does not stand in for Oxford.

**What would settle it.** Any one of: the **year**, the **college**, or a **certificate**. Letters, a passport, a dated photograph or a scholarship award among the family's papers would serve. If those papers also explain the letters "H.P." and "E.E.T&P (London)" that one local history prints after his degrees, note that too ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q3](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).

**Until then.** "The family remembers him as a travelling scholar at Oxford." Give no date and do not call him a guest lecturer.

## 4. His father's name

**Status.** Azhar remembers it as **Muhammad Sultan** *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The English scan of *The History and Culture of Bhadarwah* prints "Khwaja Sulla Bhat" (p. 774). The research quoted the OCR text layer, not the page image ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2 and source key](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). This may be a bad reading. Two other men's names could mislead a search. The book's dedication names the author's father, Khawaja Mohammad Sultan Khateeb *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. Its lists also include a "Professor Sultan Muhammad Khan" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).

**What would settle it.** Any one of:
- the page image of p. 774 in the Internet Archive item `the-history-and-culture-of-bhadarwah`, read by eye;
- the Urdu original, *Bhaderwah ki Tareekh o Saqafat*, which the research did not find;
- the service book's first page (file 3).

**Until then.** Do not print "Sulla". If the site names his father, use the family's name and say it is the family's.

## 5. Which birth date the site leads with

**Status.** The 1952 Civil List gives 26-7-1916, and two books agree ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). The family has January 1916. This is a family decision about wording. Either date can stay on record.

**What would help.** The service book's first page (file 3), or a matriculation or school-leaving certificate if the family has one.

**Until then.** Show the year alone in short places (the hero, the footer, metadata). In the biography, give both: "Government records give 26 July 1916; the family remembers January."

## 6. Masooda's age in November 1947

**Status.** The site calls her an infant. The report's summary of the first pass says she was the eldest, about two and a half, and that a six-month-old daughter was also with the family ([report: 1947 paragraph](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). No preserved quotation carries those ages ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The family's 11 November reading of the Kashmir Life obituary finds a damaged hand and a daughter killed, without a name or an age.

**What would settle it.** Family memory, or a direct reading of the 1947 passage in Khalid Bashir Ahmad's *Kashmir: A Walk Through History* (Internet Archive item `burzahom-kashmirawalkthroughhistorykhalidbashira-69086`).

**Until then.** "Their daughter, Masooda", with no age.

## 7. Warwick

**Status.** The site has him as a guest lecturer at Warwick in the 1980s. No source supports it ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)), and the family statements of 11 November 2026 do not mention Warwick.

**What would settle it.** A family check: whether the family still holds this memory and, if so, the same kind of evidence as Oxford.

**Until then.** Leave it off the site.

## 8. The early lectureship: GGM College or Mirpur

**Status.** The site has him as a lecturer at GGM Science College (then Prince of Wales College), Jammu, before 1947. No source found that post. Two local histories put his early lectureship at the Intermediate College, Mirpur ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). Both may be true.

**What would settle it.** A family check, or the service book's record of posts (file 3).

**Until then.** "Lecturer, Intermediate College, Mirpur". Add Prince of Wales College as family memory if the family confirms it.

## 10. Surviving daughters

**Status.** The Kashmir Life obituary, from one informant, says he was "survived by three sons and three daughters" ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The site lists six children in all: Masooda, who died in 1947, two surviving daughters (Yasmeen and Shaheen), and three sons. The sons match. The daughters do not. The same obituary's headline gives his age wrongly as 98 ([report: Death section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)), so it may simply be wrong here too.

**What would settle it.** A family check.

**Until then.** Keep the family's list. Leave the count out of the 2016 timeline entry.

## 11. Principal at Bhaderwah in 1956

**Status.** The Bhadarwah history makes him Principal of the Bhaderwah degree college in 1956. The Bhalessa book, which is derivative, says "principal of MP School in 1956, 57" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q3 and Q6](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). This matters only if the timeline prints the 1956 principalship.

**What would settle it.** A family check, or the service book's record of posts (file 3).

**Until then.** "Principal of the degree college at Bhaderwah", with no exact year, as in the timeline plan.

## 12. The surname in Urdu

**Status.** The site's English has read "Butt" since the family's correction of 6 January 2026 (commit `f439b8c`). The hero's Urdu still reads عبدالعزیز بھٹ, which is Bhat ([index.astro, line 19](../../src/pages/index.astro)).

**What would settle it.** A family check: how the family writes the name in Urdu.

**Until then.** Leave the Urdu as it is. Do not change it on the strength of the English spelling alone.
