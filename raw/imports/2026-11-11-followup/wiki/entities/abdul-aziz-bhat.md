# Abdul Aziz Bhat (Butt, Batt), "Abbaji"

He was an educationist from Kilhotran village in Bhalessa. He served in the state education service from 1941 to 1974. He was a professor at the university and its Registrar from September 1962 to 1971. He died in Jammu on 7 August 2016, aged 100. The memorial site spells his name **Butt**. The sources use Butt, Bhat and Batt for the same man ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q1 and Q8](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).

## Name and identity

- **Record.** The Civil Lists print "Abdul Aziz Butt". BHD prints "Professor Abdul Aziz Bhat", BHL "Professor Abdul Aziz Batt", and KBA and KL-2016 "Abdul Aziz Bhat". The Calendar prints "Prof. Abdul Aziz" with no surname ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md); [notes: documentary-gap, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).
- **Site spelling.** The site has used **Butt** since a correction on 6 January 2026. Commit `f439b8c`, "Critical corrections: surname and grandchildren section", says: change surname from "Bhat" to "Butt" throughout the entire site. The hero's Urdu, عبدالعزیز بھٹ, still reads Bhat ([index.astro, line 19](../../src/pages/index.astro)). This page's filename keeps "bhat", the spelling most of the printed sources use.
- **Family.** *Abbaji* is the family's name for him. It needs no published source *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The research found it in no public source, which is what one would expect of a family name ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2 gaps](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Namesakes.** The sources name at least nine other men called Abdul Aziz Butt or Bhat. They include a "Master Abdul Aziz Bhat" listed among other men in BHD and a "Prof. Abdul Aziz-ul-Azeem" at the University of Kashmir (a mathematician and Dean of Academic Affairs who died in 2021) ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q8](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md); [report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). Four things set him apart in the record: the birth date 26-7-1916, Kilhotran, his Education Service career and the label "former Registrar". The report also leaned on his father's name, which is now uncertain (see Father).

## Birth and family of origin

- **Birth date, record.** CL-127 gives his date of birth as 26-7-1916. BHD and KBA both give 26 July 1916 ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). KBA's statement that he died "twelve days after he had completed 100 years" fits a 26 July birth exactly ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)).
- **Birth date, family.** The site gives January 1916. The research found no published source for January ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). Both dates stay recorded. Which one the site leads with is a family decision ([open confirmations](../questions/open-confirmations.md)).
- **Birthplace, record.** BHD places it at "village Kilhotran Tehsil Bhalesa" and BHL at "Kilhotran Bhalessa (Doda)". KL-2016 places Kilhotran "in Gondu Tehsil near Thatri" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md); [first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)). All three agree with the family's Bhalessa.
- **Father, record.** The English OCR text of BHD, p. 774, reads "born … to **Khwaja Sulla Bhat**" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). The research quoted the OCR text layer, not the page image ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, source key](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). BHL adds that "his father died in 1960 and mother four years later". BHL is derivative ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Father, family.** Azhar remembers his father's name as **Muhammad Sultan** *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. "Sulla" may be a bad reading of the scan. The book's dedication names the author's father, Khawaja Mohammad Sultan Khateeb. He is a different man *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. BHD also names a "Professor Sultan Muhammad Khan" among noted men of the region, a third person ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2 and Q3](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). **Status: unsettled. Do not print "Sulla" as his father's name.** The report's suggested line "son of Khwaja Sulla Bhat" ([report: memorial table](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)) is withdrawn for site use.
- **Siblings, record.** BHL names brothers Nizamud Din and Gh. Qadir Bhat ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).

## Schooling and degrees

- **Record.** He went to the primary school at Kilhotran (BHL p. 28; the name in that list is ambiguous). He was in "the first group from Bhalesa that took admission in Bhadarwah School", and he was Ghulam Rasool Azad's classmate "since childhood until the 10[th] Class" (BHD). BHD also calls him "the first M.A. of this place" (pp. 670–671) ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2 and Q3](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Record.** He "passed his BA from Prince of Wales College Jammu in 1938" (BHD p. 774). The college is now Government Gandhi Memorial (GGM) Science College. Greater Kashmir (2015) makes the link; the renaming date of 20 May 1948 comes only from search-result text ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** "In his own words", he completed an "MA Arabic and LLB course from Aligarh Muslim University". He passed the B.T. (Bachelor of Teaching) and cleared Munshi Fazil in the first class (BHD p. 774). BHL lists "M.A, B.T, L.L.B, H.P., E.E.T&P (London)", and the research found no explanation for the last two ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q3](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). BHD also lists him among the Persian experts of the area ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Family.** He taught himself to read as a child, and by the family's count he was one of only fourteen people from his village to be educated (the site's existing copy). No record conflicts with either.
- **Reading.** The site's "GGM Science College" is the same college, under its later name. **He was not a student at the Bhaderwah degree college.** By BHD he was its Principal in 1956, and its founding year (1955, from search-result text only) comes long after his 1938 B.A. ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q3](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). **No source found a doctorate**, and both degree lists leave one out ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). Inference from the notes: the Bhaderwah college memory probably joins his schooling at Bhaderwah to his later principalship there.

## Work before 1947

- **Record.** CL-127 dates his entry into government service to 23-2-1998 Bikrami, roughly May–June 1941, and his promotion to the headmasters' grade to about March 1945. The researchers converted these dates approximately ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). BHD says he was appointed "Second-Teacher in Kishtwar High School in the year 1941 AD" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Record.** KBA, from his own interview: "Head Master of Hari Singh High School and later Registrar of Jammu & Kashmir University" ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The 1947 Civil List probably shows him as Head Master, Government High School, Kishtwar. The researchers rate that match by column position as probable, not certain ([report: Civil lists section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** "One of his earliest postings was as Lecturer at Intermediate College, Mirpur" (BHL p. 32). BHD's damaged text mentions "Lecturer and Headmaster at Mirpur" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Family.** He was Principal of Hari Singh High School and a Lecturer at GGM Science College, Jammu, both before 1947 (the site's existing copy).
- **Reading.** The Hari Singh headship agrees with his own account. The record's title is Head Master, not Principal. No source found the GGM lectureship. The lectureship on record is at Mirpur. That is a gap in the record, not a disproof, so it goes to the family as a question ([open confirmations](../questions/open-confirmations.md)).

## November 1947

- **Record.** BHD's OCR is broken here. The legible fragments say that during the 1947 riots he and his wife **Syeda** were to be taken to Pakistan in a caravan from Jammu. The caravan was attacked "on 5[th] and 6 November 1947 AD" some distance from Jammu. "His daughter fell Prey to the bullets of rioters", and the couple survived (BHD pp. 774–775). BHL says only "He lost her daughter in turmoil" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). KL-2016's headline calls him a witness to the "1947 Massacre Of Jammu Muslims" ([report: 1947 paragraph](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record, as read by the family.** The KL-2016 obituary says his hand was damaged and a daughter was killed. It does not mention the thumb or name Masooda *(family reading of KL-2016, 11 Nov 2026)*.
- **Family.** The daughter was **Masooda** *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. Azhar grew up with Abbaji's thumb missing *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. The site's existing copy adds the family's further details: the family was put on a bus said to be going to safety, and it went into an ambush. He was holding Masooda when he was shot, and the bullet that killed her took his left thumb. His wife, Saideh, survived a head wound.
- **Reading.** Keep both the name and the thumb. They are family knowledge and fit the record's outline. **Correction to the report:** the report's summary says the first pass took the name Masooda, a hand wound, and the girls' ages (about two and a half, and a second daughter of six months) from KL-2016 and KBA ([report: 1947 paragraph](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). The family's 11 November reading of KL-2016 does not find the name there. No preserved quotation in the research carries the name or the ages ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The name rests on the family. The ages are an open family question.
- **Wife's name.** The family spells it Saideh (or Saida). BHD prints "Syeda" and "Syeda Ji" and says she died "a few years ago", counting back from an undated Urdu original ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). Inference: the same name, spelled differently.

## Kashmir, 1948–1962

- **Record.** BHD: Inspector of Schools in Anantnag. In CL-127 (about October 1952), the post that lines up with his entry by position is "Assistant Inspector of Schools, Anantnag" ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). All three Civil Lists keep him in the state school cadre from 1947 to about 1953 ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** In 1956 "he also completed a … Training Course in England along with Ghulam Rasool Azad and others" (BHD p. 775). BHD also makes him "Principal of Bhadarwah Degree College" in 1956. BHL instead has him "principal of MP School in 1956, 57", later principal of S.P. College ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q6 and Q7](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Record.** KBA: Secretary of the J&K Board of School Education, Principal of S.P. College and Inspector of Schools. BHL calls the Board post "Chairman". BHL is derivative, so "Secretary" is the safer word. BHD: "later also appointed as the Deputy Director of Education Department Kashmir", just before the registrarship ([report: Civil lists section, closing paragraph](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)).
- **Family.** He was a **travelling scholar at Oxford** *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. No opened source mentions Oxford ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q7](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). **Status: family knowledge, waiting on a year, a college or a certificate.** The 1956 England course is a separate documented fact and does **not** replace Oxford. Neither one explains the other until a document links them. The year of the Oxford visit is unknown, so this page gives it no date.
- **Earlier site copy, not in the 11 Nov statements.** The site calls him a guest lecturer at Oxford and at **Warwick** in the 1980s. No source supports Warwick or the 1980s date ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)), and the 11 November statements do not mention Warwick. Open family question.

## At the university, 1962–1971

- **Record.** The Calendar's Registrar list, read from the page image, says "4. Prof. Abdul Aziz — Sept1962 – 1971". He follows Prof. A. S. Dhar (May–Sept 1962) and comes before Prof. M. A. Chishti (1971–1975). The PDF's text layer garbles the name as "Abdul Psiz", so text searches miss it ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md); [report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** BHD and KBA give Registrar from 1962 to 1969, continuing at the University of Kashmir after the split. BHD dates his retirement from government service to 1974. KL-2016 and BHL put his departure in 1966. GK-2021: "Professor Abdul Aziz served as the Registrar & Controller of the Jammu & Kashmir University, he was a great administrator" ([report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md); [education first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-first-pass-excerpt.md)). The Calendar's dates govern. In the 1962–69 account, 1969 marks the division of the university, not his departure, and 1966 is the outlier ([report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** The 1965 reorganisation created a Kashmir Division. A 1966 university journal names a "Joint-Registrar, … Kashmir Division". The Calendar's single line may therefore cover more than one title ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1 inferences](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)). Under s.52 of the 1969 Act, staff of the old university carried over to the new ones ([notes: founding, §4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md)). Under s.17, the Registrar was a whole-time administrative officer. He was secretary to the councils, faculties and boards, and he kept the records and the seal ([notes: education-and-registrar, Q4](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-and-registrar.md)).
- **Professor, record.** The Calendar, BHD, BHL and GK-2021 all give him the title Professor. **No source names a department or subject** ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). The research notes offer two inferences, neither of them a finding: one reads "Prof." as implying a teaching post ([notes: documentary-gap, Q2 gaps](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)), and the other says the title might be honorific ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q6 inferences](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **Professor, family.** He was a professor at the University of Kashmir *(family, 11 Nov 2026)*. **Status: the professorship stands. The department and the appointment paper are unconfirmed.** The record does not show it to be false.
- **Identification.** The family knows he was the Registrar. What is missing is a university document with his surname. The Calendar line has none. The link from that line to him rests on KBA, BHD, KL-2016 and GK-2021 agreeing. Three university files would close the gap ([open confirmations](../questions/open-confirmations.md); [report: Conclusion](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).

## What the record does not support

These claims come from the earlier site copy. The 11 November family statements do not make them.

- **Founding faculty, 1948.** In 1948 the state set up "an institute for conduct of examinations". The university began teaching in 1956 (1958 in one account), and the Civil Lists keep him in the school cadre until about 1953 ([notes: founding, §1–2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md); [report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **"From the rock".** The phrase appears in none of the official pages or newspaper histories opened. The motto is translated "From Darkness to Light". Building on the Hazratbal campus began in 1959 ([notes: founding, §5](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/founding.md)).
- **Built the Education department or its curriculum; Dean of Education.** The Department of Education dates itself to 1965. A 1964–66 university journal names "Dr. Ashraf Agha, Head of Post-graduate Department of Education". Later tributes credit Agha Ashraf Ali as founder Dean and Head ([notes: documentary-gap, Q2](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md); [notes: education-and-registrar, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/education-and-registrar.md)). It is an inference that the two names belong to one man ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Professor of English, Education or Social Work.** No source names a subject. Social Work was founded in 2003 ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q6](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)).
- **PhD in Education.** Not found ([report: Education department section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Registrar "in the 1970s".** The record has September 1962 to 1971 ([notes: documentary-gap, Q1](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/documentary-gap.md)).

## After 1971

- **Record.** He retired from government service in 1974 (BHD). From 1974 to 1981 he was Principal of the Muslim Educational Trust's College of Education at Sopore, a teachers' college (KBA; BHL). BHD gives "the next five years". In 1986 he "laid the foundation of College of Education in Model Institute of Education Jammu" and became adviser to its director (BHD p. 775) ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q6](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). Nothing found covers 1971–1974 ([report: Calendar section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** He was at a felicitation for Ghulam Nabi Azad in Jammu on 8 February 2006 (BHD) and gave KBA an interview in December 2011 ([report: Death section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Family.** He did social work with young people throughout his life (the site's existing copy).

## Death

- **Record.** He died in Jammu on **7 August 2016**, aged 100. KL-2016 (8 August 2016) says "he breathed his last in Jammu on Sunday", and 7 August 2016 was a Sunday. KBA says he died "twelve days after he had completed 100 years". KL-2016's headline figure of "98" is an error ([report: Death section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)).
- **Record.** KNS-2017 (7 April 2017) announces the death of "Master Abdul Aziz Bhat" and gives no age, place or posts. BHL copies its opening sentence and changes "Master" to "Professor". **The notice does not identify him** ([report: Death section](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/report.md)). The site's quotation "Ever-smiling, soft spoken, meticulously dressed…" comes from this notice ([notes: abdul-aziz-butt, Q7](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt.md)). See [site content updates](../topics/site-content-updates.md).

## Children

- **Record.** KL-2016, from one informant: "survived by three sons and three daughters. His son, Javid Ahmad, is first Muslim commercial pilot of Kashmir. Another son, Altaf Ahmad, retired as executive engineer and third son is a doctor" ([first-pass excerpt](../../research/2026-11-11-founding/notes/abdul-aziz-butt-first-pass-excerpt.md)).
- **Family.** Six children: Masooda (died 1947), Yasmeen, Altaf (civil engineer), Javaid (pilot), Shaheen, and Afzal (born 1955, general surgeon) (the site's existing copy).
- **Reading.** The sons agree. The daughters may not: KL-2016 says three daughters survived him, while the family list has two surviving daughters besides Masooda. Open family question.
