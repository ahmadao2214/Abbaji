# Family statements, 11 November 2026

Azhar, Abbaji's grandson, gave these statements to the agents working on the wiki. They are family knowledge. A missing document does not disprove them.

## First round (task brief)

- **Professor.** He was a professor at the University of Kashmir. The department and the appointment paper are not known.
- **Oxford.** He was a travelling scholar at Oxford. The year, college and certificate are not known. This is not the 1956 England course.
- **Father.** His father's name is Muhammad Sultan. "Khwaja Sulla Bhat" is not settled. The book's dedication names a different man, Khawaja Mohammad Sultan Khateeb, the author's father.
- **1947.** His thumb was missing, and the daughter killed was Masooda. The family's reading of the Kashmir Life obituary of 8 August 2016 is that it mentions a damaged hand and a daughter killed, but names neither the thumb nor Masooda.
- **Abbaji.** This is the family's name for him and needs no published source.

## Second round (comments on the agent's summary)

- **Birth date.** Of the record's 26 July 1916 and the site's January 1916: "This is probably the correct" (said of 26 July).
- **Village.** Of the site's "one of only fourteen people from his village to be educated": he was the "first educated person from his village".
- **Urdu surname.** On the hero's Urdu spelling, which reads Bhat, Azhar asked: "is it butt?" This is a question, not a statement. The answer is recorded on the wiki's open-confirmations page.
